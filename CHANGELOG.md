## 0.0.3

* **Drop-in Parity:** Added all standard `GridView` constructors: `EzGridView.new`, `EzGridView.count`, `EzGridView.extent`, and `EzGridView.custom` alongside `EzGridView.builder`.
* **Standard Properties:** Added full support for `scrollDirection`, `reverse`, `controller`, `primary`, `physics`, `shrinkWrap`, `padding`, `cacheExtent`, `semanticChildCount`, `dragStartBehavior`, `keyboardDismissBehavior`, `restorationId`, `clipBehavior`, and `hitTestBehavior`.
* **Layout Fallback & Diagnostics:** Added `fallbackWidth`, `fallbackHeight`, `showDebugIndicator`, and `onUnboundedDetected` callback for custom telemetry and testing.
* **Testing & Examples:** Added comprehensive widget test suite and modernized example showcase.
